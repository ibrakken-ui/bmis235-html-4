Our Process

Organizing shouldn't feel overwhelming. Here's how every Indie & Co. project works.

1. Free Consultation

We talk through your space, your goals, and what frustrates you most. You'll leave with a clear plan and a quote, with no obligation.

2. Sort & Declutter

Together we decide what to keep, donate, or toss, with no pressure and no judgment.

3. Design the System

We choose containers, labels, and layouts that fit your habits.

4. Maintain

You get a simple 10-minute weekly reset routine so the space stays organized.
